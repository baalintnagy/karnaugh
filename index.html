Notation & conventions

Inputs run from A to H, with A as the most significant bit. Rows ascend from 0 to 2ⁿ−1. The function order is Σ f(i)2ⁱ. DNF and CNF are canonical forms. Minimal is a minimum sum of products, ranked by term count then literal count. NAND and NOR use minimized SOP and POS respectively. Gates may have multiple inputs. X means don't care: the solver can choose 0 or 1 for that row. Each circuit satisfies all specified rows.
