Notation & conventions

Inputs run from A to H, with A as the most significant bit. Rows ascend from 0 to 2ⁿ−1. The function order is Σ f(i)2ⁱ. DNF and CNF are canonical forms. Minimal is a minimum sum of products, ranked by term count then literal count. NAND and NOR use minimized SOP and POS respectively. XOR is the algebraic normal form (AND and XOR). Gates may have multiple inputs. No don't-care values are used.
